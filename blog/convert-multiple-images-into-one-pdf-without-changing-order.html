Common Mistakes & Why They Happen

Even with a simple tool, mistakes can happen. Here are the most common ones and why they occur.

⚠️ Mistake 1: Selecting images in the wrong order

Why it happens: You select all files using Ctrl+A or by clicking the first file and then Shift+Click on the last file. This often results in alphabetical or reverse alphabetical order.

Fix: Always select files sequentially — click the first file, then Ctrl+Click (or Cmd+Click) on each subsequent file in the correct order.

⚠️ Mistake 2: Using inconsistent file names

Why it happens: You name your files randomly — img1.jpg, image_2.jpg, scan3.jpg. This creates confusion and makes it hard to select them in order.

Fix: Use a consistent naming pattern with leading zeros for numbers. Example: page_01.jpg, page_02.jpg, page_03.jpg … page_20.jpg.

⚠️ Mistake 3: Not checking the preview before conversion

Why it happens: You're in a hurry and just click "Convert" without verifying the order. Then you download the PDF and realize the pages are shuffled.

Fix: Always check the preview list of uploaded images. If the order is wrong, remove the images and re-upload them correctly.

⚠️ Mistake 4: Using low-resolution images

Why it happens: You use images that are already compressed or low-quality. The resulting PDF will also be low-quality.

Fix: Always use the highest quality images available. If you're scanning documents, scan at 300 DPI or higher.
