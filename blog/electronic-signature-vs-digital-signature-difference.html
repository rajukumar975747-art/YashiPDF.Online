Electronic Signature vs Digital Signature: What's the Difference?

By Raju  |  11/jul/26  |  YashiPDF.online

Ek clear comparison jo aapko batayega ki kaunsa signature type aapke document ke liye sahi hai

Quick Answer: An electronic signature (e-signature) is any digital mark showing intent to sign — a typed name, scanned signature image, or a click on "I Agree." A digital signature is a specific, cryptographically secured type of electronic signature that uses encryption (PKI technology) to verify signer identity and detect tampering. In short: all digital signatures are electronic signatures, but not all electronic signatures are digital signatures.

Agar aap kisi PDF par sign karne wale hain — chahe wo job offer letter ho, rental agreement ho, ya koi business contract — toh ek sawal zaroor aata hai: "Simple e-sign kaafi hai ya mujhe digital signature use karni chahiye?" Yeh confusion itni common hai ki bahut saare log dono terms ko interchangeably use kar dete hain, jabki technically dono bilkul alag cheez hain.

Is article mein hum teeno tareeke dekhenge jinse aap document sign kar sakte hain — online web tool, desktop software, aur mobile app — aur samjhenge ki har method kis situation ke liye best hai. Chahe aap ek freelancer ho jo client contract sign kar raha hai, ya ek company jo legal documents process kar rahi hai, is guide ke end tak aapko exactly pata chal jayega ki kaunsa signature method choose karna hai.

Why Different Methods Give Different Results

Signature ka purpose hamesha same hota hai — kisi document par apni consent, approval, ya agreement dikhana. Lekin har method ka underlying technology alag hota hai, aur isi wajah se legal validity, security level, aur trust factor bhi alag-alag hota hai.

Electronic signatures broad category hai jisme kuch bhi aa sakta hai jo "sign" ka intent dikhaye — ek scanned image, typed name, ya drawing pad se banaya gaya signature. Yeh convenient hote hain lekin inme built-in security verification nahi hoti. Koi bhi Photoshop ya basic editing tool se aisa signature copy kar sakta hai.

Digital signatures dusri taraf ek technical process follow karte hain jise Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) kehte hain. Jab aap digitally sign karte hain, ek unique cryptographic hash generate hota hai jo document ke content se directly linked hota hai. Agar document mein signing ke baad ek bhi character change hota hai, signature invalid ho jata hai. Yeh tamper-evidence hi digital signature ko itna trusted banata hai legal aur financial documents ke liye.

Yeh difference samajhna zaroori hai kyunki galat method choose karne se aapka document legally weak ho sakta hai, ya phir aap unnecessary complexity mein fasa sakte hain jab simple e-sign bhi kaafi hota.

Method 1: Online Web-Based E-Signature Tool

Online tools jaise DocuSign, Adobe Sign, HelloSign, ya YashiPDF ke sign tools browser mein directly kaam karte hain. Aapko koi software install karne ki zaroorat nahi hoti — bas PDF upload karo, signature add karo, aur download kar lo.

Steps to Sign Using an Online Tool

  1. Apne browser mein signing tool website open karein.
  2. Jis PDF ya document par sign karna hai, use upload karein.
  3. Signature field ya blank space par click karke apna signature draw, type, ya image upload karke add karein.
  4. Agar zaroori ho toh date, initials, ya checkbox fields bhi fill karein.
  5. Signed document ko save ya download karein, ya directly recipient ko email karein.

✅ Pros

  • Koi installation nahi, kahin bhi kaam karta hai
  • Fast aur beginner-friendly
  • Multiple devices se access kar sakte hain
  • Kai tools mein audit trail aur tracking feature milta hai

❌ Cons

  • Simple e-sign tools mein cryptographic verification nahi hoti
  • Sensitive legal documents ke liye kabhi-kabhi kaafi nahi hota
  • Free tools mein feature limits ho sakte hain
  • Internet connection zaroori hota hai

Agar aapko kisi form ko pehle fillable banana ho taaki signature field properly kaam kare, toh hamara guide dekh sakte hain: How to create fillable PDF forms.

Method 2: Desktop Software (Certificate-Based Digital Signature)

Desktop software jaise Adobe Acrobat Pro, ya DSC (Digital Signature Certificate) tools India mein government aur legal work ke liye use hote hain. Yeh true digital signatures create karte hain using a certificate issued by a licensed Certifying Authority (CA).

Steps to Sign Using Desktop Software

  1. Apna digital signature certificate (USB token ya software-based) system mein install/connect karein.
  2. Acrobat Pro ya similar software mein document open karein.
  3. "Certificates" ya "Digital Signature" tool select karein.
  4. Signature field draw karein aur apna certificate select karein.
  5. PIN enter karke sign confirm karein — is se cryptographic hash generate hota hai.
  6. Document save karein; ab yeh tamper-evident ban chuka hai.

✅ Pros

  • Highest level of legal validity aur security
  • Tamper detection built-in
  • Government filings, tax documents, tenders ke liye required
  • Signer identity verified by a trusted authority

❌ Cons

  • DSC token/certificate purchase karna padta hai (cost involved)
  • Setup thoda technical hota hai beginners ke liye
  • Software installation zaroori hoti hai
  • Certificate expire hone par renew karna padta hai
Tip: Agar aap government tenders, GST filings, ya company incorporation documents sign kar rahe hain, tab digital signature certificate (Class 2 ya Class 3 DSC) hi legally accepted hota hai — simple e-sign yahan kaam nahi karega.

Method 3: Mobile App / Browser-Based Signing

Mobile apps jaise Adobe Fill & Sign, CamScanner, ya YashiPDF ke mobile-friendly web tools on-the-go signing ke liye perfect hain. Yeh mostly electronic signature category mein aate hain, unless specifically DSC-integrated ho.

Steps to Sign Using a Mobile App

  1. App open karein aur document (photo ya PDF) upload karein.
  2. Signature tool select karke touchscreen par sign draw karein.
  3. Signature ko resize aur position karein document par.
  4. Document save karein aur directly WhatsApp, email, ya cloud storage se share karein.

✅ Pros

  • Sabse fast method jab aap travel kar rahe ho
  • Touch-based signing natural feel deta hai
  • Instant sharing options available

❌ Cons

  • Small screen par precise signing mushkil ho sakta hai
  • Zyadatar apps true cryptographic digital signature support nahi karte
  • Legal-grade documents ke liye risky ho sakta hai

Which Method Is Best For You?

Agar aap ek freelancer ya small business owner ho jo client agreements ya invoices sign kar rahe ho, toh online e-signature tool sabse practical option hai — fast, free, aur zyada tar cases mein legally sufficient bhi.

Agar aapko government tenders, income tax filings, MCA (company registration) documents, ya any legally binding certificate submit karna hai, toh digital signature certificate (DSC) hi sahi choice hai. Yahan compromise karna risky ho sakta hai kyunki authorities sirf verified DSC accept karte hain.

Agar aap kahin bahar ho aur turant kisi form par sign karke bhejna hai — jaise delivery acknowledgment ya simple consent form — mobile app fastest route hai.

Simple Rule: Personal aur business convenience ke liye electronic signature use karein. Legal, government, ya financial compliance ke liye digital signature certificate use karein.

Common Mistakes and Their Solutions

  • Mistake: Legal contract par simple scanned signature use karna. Solution: High-value contracts ke liye digital signature ya at least a verified e-sign platform use karein jo audit trail deta ho.
  • Mistake: DSC token ka PIN bhool jana ya expired certificate use karna. Solution: Certificate expiry date calendar mein set karein aur renewal time se pehle karein.
  • Mistake: Mobile par signature itna chhota banana ki wo blurry dikhe. Solution: Signature ko zoom karke precise draw karein, ya pehle se saved signature image use karein.
  • Mistake: Signature field ko galat page ya position par place karna. Solution: Sign karne se pehle document properly preview karein.

Best Practices for Signing Documents

  • Har important document sign karne se pehle content ache se padhein.
  • Legal ya financial documents ke liye hamesha verified/certified method use karein.
  • Signed copy ka backup securely store karein — cloud aur local dono jagah.
  • Agar document mein sensitive information hai, sign karne se pehle metadata check karein. Iske liye yeh guide helpful hai: How to remove metadata from images before sharing.

Final Thoughts

Electronic signature aur digital signature dono apni jagah useful hain — bas context alag hota hai. Roz-marra ke business documents, approvals, aur agreements ke liye electronic signature kaafi hota hai. Lekin jab baat legal compliance, government filings, ya high-value contracts ki ho, digital signature certificate hi safe aur required option hai. Sahi method choose karna aapke document ki legal strength aur trust factor decide karta hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an electronic signature legally valid in India?

Haan, electronic signatures ko IT Act 2000 ke under legal recognition mila hai, lekin unki validity strength document ke type aur context par depend karti hai.

Do I need a digital signature certificate for every document?

Nahi. Digital signature certificate mainly government filings, tenders, aur legally sensitive documents ke liye required hoti hai. Normal business agreements ke liye electronic signature kaafi hoti hai.

Can I convert a scanned signature into a digital signature?

Nahi directly. Ek scanned image sirf ek visual mark hoti hai. True digital signature banane ke liye aapko ek certified digital signature certificate (DSC) ki zaroorat hoti hai jo cryptographically document ko secure kare.

Which is more secure — electronic or digital signature?

Digital signature zyada secure hoti hai kyunki isme encryption aur tamper detection built-in hota hai. Electronic signature convenient hoti hai lekin utni technically secure nahi hoti.

Can I use a mobile app to create a legally valid signature?

Zyadatar mobile apps electronic signature create karte hain jo casual aur business use ke liye theek hoti hai, lekin legal-grade documents ke liye desktop-based DSC signing zyada reliable hoti hai.
