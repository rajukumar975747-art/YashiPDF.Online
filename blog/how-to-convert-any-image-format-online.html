What You Should Know Before Using This Tool

Image format convert karne pehle kuch important baatein samajhna zaroori hai:

Different Formats Have Different Uses:

  • JPG: Photos ke liye best, small size, lossy compression
  • PNG: Documents, screenshots, logos ke liye best, full quality, lossless
  • WebP: Modern websites ke liye, lekin purane devices support nahi karte
  • HEIC: iPhone photos ka format, lekin purane systems support nahi karte

PNG se JPG convert karne se quality thodi kam ho sakti (JPG lossy hai). JPG se PNG convert karne se quality maintain rehta. Isliye sahi format choose karein apne use ke according.
