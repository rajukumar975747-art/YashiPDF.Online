7 Expert Tips for Perfect JPG to PDF Conversion

  • Tip 1: Image Quality High Rakhein — Low quality JPG se PDF bhi low quality hoga. Original high-resolution JPG use karein.
  • Tip 2: Correct Order Mein Arrange Karein — Multiple images ko drag & drop se sahi sequence mein rakhein (Page 1, Page 2, Page 3...).
  • Tip 3: File Size Check Karein — Government form upload ke liye PDF size typically 100KB-500KB hona chahiye. Zyada badi file upload fail ho sakti hai.
  • Tip 4: White Background Preferred — Documents ke liye white background JPG better PDF deta hai. Colored background avoid karein.
  • Tip 5: JPG Format Standard Ho — Standard JPG/JPEG format use karein. Rare formats (WEBP, TIFF) pehle JPG mein convert karein.
  • Tip 6: Test Download Karein — PDF convert karne ke baad download karein aur open karke check karein ki sabhi pages correct hain.
  • Tip 7: Browser Cache Clear Karein — Agar website slow kaam kar rahi hai, toh browser cache clear karein (Settings > Privacy > Clear Cache).

5 Common Mistakes That Ruin Your PDF Conversion

  1. ❌ Low Quality JPG: Blurry ya pixelated JPG se PDF bhi blurry hoga — government form reject ho sakta hai.
  2. ❌ Wrong Order: Multiple pages ko galat sequence mein arrange kiya — document samajh nahi aayega.
  3. ❌ File Size Too Large: 5MB PDF jab limit 500KB hai — upload fail ho jayega.
  4. ❌ Corrupted JPG: Damaged/corrupted JPG file upload ki — conversion fail ho jata hai.
  5. ❌ Internet Connection Slow: Weak internet par upload kar diya — process interrupt ho gaya.

⚠️ Warning: Ek mistake bhi aapki sari application waste kar sakti hai. Har detail double-check karein before submitting.
