When to Use PNG vs JPG? (Complete Comparison)

Har format ki apni khasiyat hoti hai. Kab PNG use karein aur kab JPG – yeh samajhna zaroori hai:

✅ PNG Format Use Karein Jab:

  • Image mein transparency chahiye (logo, icons)
  • High quality maintain karna zaroori hai (professional photography)
  • Text ya screenshots save kar rahe hain
  • Image ko baar-baar edit karna hai (no quality loss)
  • Web design aur graphics work hai

✅ JPG Format Use Karein Jab:
