UPSC Photo & Signature Requirements (short)

Answer-first: UPSC wants a clear passport-style photo and a handwritten signature scanned properly. Key points:

  • Format: JPG/JPEG (preferred), sometimes PNG accepted — use JPG for smaller KB.
  • Background: Plain light (white/very light grey). No shadows or patterns.
  • Face: Full front-facing head and shoulders, eyes open, neutral expression; no sunglasses or heavy filters.
  • Signature: Sign with black/blue pen on white paper, scan/crop tightly, avoid smudges.
  • File size: Follow the notification — commonly photo 20–100 KB, signature 10–50 KB.

Step-by-step resize & compress guide (fast method)

Follow this sequence for reliable output. Use local tools (Photos/Preview/GIMP) or client-side web tools like YashiPDF.Online to keep data private.

  1. Scan or photograph: Use a smartphone camera in natural light. Keep background plain and steady the camera.
  2. Crop tightly: Include head and upper shoulders, leave small margin above head.
  3. Resize to recommended pixels: Open image editor and set to 354 × 472 px (or 600 × 800 px). Maintain aspect ratio.
  4. Adjust brightness/contrast: Make face natural and background even; avoid heavy sharpening or filters.
  5. Export as JPG: Choose quality 70–85% to keep details but reduce KB.
  6. Check file size: If >100 KB, lower quality to 60–70% or reduce pixels slightly; if <20 KB and too soft, increase quality/pixels.
  7. Signature: Scan signature at 300 DPI, crop tightly, resize to 200 × 100 px, save as JPG with quality 80%.

Real examples (scenario-based)

Three short cases you will meet:

  • Case A — Photo too large (5 MB): Resize to 600×800 px, set JPG quality 78% → file 60 KB; crop to face and re-export.
  • Case B — Low-res photo (15 KB, blurry): Re-scan or take new photo; increasing pixels won’t recover detail — take new clear photo.
  • Case C — Signature faint after compression: Re-scan at higher contrast, increase quality to 85% and crop tightly; re-export to target KB.

Expert tips & common mistakes

Expert tips

  • Always keep an original uncompressed copy — you may need it later.
  • Prefer JPG baseline; use progressive JPEG only if system accepts it.
  • For official forms, avoid filters, hats, or decorative borders.
  • If site shows exact KB ranges, aim for middle — e.g., if allowed 10–100 KB, target 30–50 KB for safety.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Uploading screenshots or WhatsApp-compressed images — these lose quality.
  • Using heavy auto-enhance that changes face features or color balance.
  • Submitting signatures with lines cut off or shaky scans.
  • Ignoring the official notification — KB limits may vary by year/post.

When to use / when not to use

  • Use high-DPI scanned photo when the form clearly states DPI or requires print-quality image.
  • Use lower pixel targets (but clear) when the portal enforces strict KB limits.
  • Do not upscale a tiny blurry image — always re-take or re-scan.

FAQ

Q: What if my uploaded photo is rejected?

A: Check the rejection reason — usually background, file size, or face not visible. Correct using the steps above and re-upload before the deadline.

Q: Can I use a colored background?

A: Prefer plain light background. Some postings may require white only; check the notification.

Q: Does DPI matter for the online form?

A: DPI matters if the portal expects print-quality scans. For online display, pixel dimensions and clarity matter more than DPI.

Q: Can I take the photo on my phone?

A: Yes — modern phones are fine. Use natural light, plain background, crop properly and avoid beautify filters.

Q: Are PNG files accepted?

A: UPSC usually prefers JPG/JPEG. PNG can be larger and may not be accepted by every portal.
